FACULTY SALARY AND LEAVE POLICY - 2026-27

1. Salary levels
Assistant Professor: pay level 10, starting basic pay Rs 57,700 per month.
Associate Professor: pay level 13A, starting basic pay Rs 1,31,400 per month.
Professor: pay level 14, starting basic pay Rs 1,44,200 per month.

2. Annual increment
The annual salary increment of 3% is given every year on 1 July, after a satisfactory performance appraisal.

3. Research allowance
Faculty members who publish in a Scopus-indexed journal receive a research allowance of Rs 25,000 per paper, up to 4 papers a year.

4. Leave for faculty
Casual leave: 8 days per academic year.
Earned leave: 15 days per academic year, which can be carried forward up to 60 days.
Medical leave: 10 days per academic year with a medical certificate.
Sabbatical leave: one year of paid sabbatical after 6 years of continuous service, for research or higher studies.

5. Applying for leave
Leave is applied for on the staff portal at least 3 working days in advance, except medical leave. The Head of Department approves casual leave; the Dean approves earned leave and sabbatical leave.
