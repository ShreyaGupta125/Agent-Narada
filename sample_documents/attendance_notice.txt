ATTENDANCE NOTICE - ODD SEMESTER 2026-27
Issued by: Office of the Dean of Academics
Date of notice: 5 October 2026

1. Minimum attendance
Every student must attend at least 75% of the classes in each course, counted separately for lectures, tutorials and labs.

2. Debarment from examinations
A student whose attendance is below 75% in a course is debarred from the end-semester examination of that course. The list of debarred students will be published on 20 November 2026.

3. Condonation of attendance shortage
A student whose attendance is between 65% and 75% may apply for condonation. The condonation fee is Rs 2,000 per course. Applications must reach the Attendance Cell by 25 November 2026. Students below 65% cannot apply for condonation.

4. Medical leave
Medical leave is counted for attendance only if a medical certificate is submitted to the programme office within 7 days of returning to classes. Medical leave can cover at most 10% of the classes in a course.

5. Late arrival
A student who arrives more than 10 minutes after a class starts is marked absent for that class.

6. Checking your attendance
Attendance is shown on the student portal and is updated every Friday. Report any mistake to the course teacher within 3 working days.

7. Contact
Attendance Cell, Block 5, Room 104. Email: attendance.cell@example.edu. Open Monday to Friday, 9:30 am to 4:30 pm.
