HOSTEL RULES FOR STUDENTS - 2026-27

1. Gate timings
The hostel gates close at 10:00 pm every night and open at 5:30 am. A student returning after 10:00 pm must enter the late entry in the register at the gate and inform the warden.

2. Night out and leave
A student who wants to stay outside overnight must apply for a night-out pass on the hostel portal by 6:00 pm on the same day, with permission from a parent.

3. Visitors
Visitors may meet students only in the visitors' lounge, from 4:00 pm to 7:00 pm. Visitors are not allowed in the rooms.

4. Mess timings
Breakfast: 7:30 am to 9:30 am. Lunch: 12:30 pm to 2:30 pm. Snacks: 5:00 pm to 6:00 pm. Dinner: 7:30 pm to 9:30 pm.

5. Electrical appliances
Electric kettles, heaters and induction cooktops are not allowed in rooms. A first offence is fined Rs 1,000 and the appliance is taken away.

6. Room cleaning and maintenance
Rooms are cleaned on Monday and Thursday. Maintenance complaints are raised on the hostel portal and are attended within 48 hours.

7. Wardens
Boys' hostel warden: Mr. Sanjay Kulkarni, extension 4100. Girls' hostel warden: Ms. Kavita Joshi, extension 4200. (Fictional names.)
