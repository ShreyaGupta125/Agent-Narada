FEE STRUCTURE - EVEN SEMESTER (JANUARY TO MAY 2027)

1. Tuition fee per semester
B.Tech (all branches): Rs 2,10,000.
BBA: Rs 1,45,000.
B.Des: Rs 1,90,000.

2. Other fees per semester
Examination fee: Rs 4,500.
Library and laboratory fee: Rs 6,000.
Hostel fee (twin sharing, with mess): Rs 95,000.
Hostel fee (single room, with mess): Rs 1,25,000.
Transport fee (optional): Rs 18,000.

3. Due date
The fee for the even semester must be paid by 15 January 2027.

4. Late fee
A late fee of Rs 100 per day is charged from 16 January 2027. After 31 January 2027 the student's registration for the semester is put on hold.

5. How to pay
Fees are paid online on the student portal, by net banking, UPI or card. Payment by cash is not accepted.

6. Scholarships
Merit scholarship holders pay the tuition fee after the scholarship is deducted. Scholarship queries go to the Scholarship Cell, Block 1, Room 110, email scholarship.cell@example.edu

7. Refunds
A student who withdraws before 31 January 2027 gets back 80% of the tuition fee. No refund is given after that date.
