CENTRAL LIBRARY RULES - 2026-27

1. Library timings
Monday to Friday: 8:00 am to 10:00 pm.
Saturday: 9:00 am to 5:00 pm.
Sunday and university holidays: closed.
During the end-semester examinations the library stays open until midnight.

2. Borrowing books
Students may borrow up to 4 books at a time, for 14 days.
Teaching staff may borrow up to 10 books at a time, for 30 days.
Non-teaching staff may borrow up to 3 books at a time, for 14 days.
A book can be renewed once, if nobody else has reserved it.

3. Fines
A book returned late is fined Rs 5 per day. A lost book must be replaced, or paid for at twice its price.

4. Reference books and journals
Reference books, journals and theses cannot be taken out of the library. They may be read inside the reading hall.

5. Digital library
E-books and research databases can be used from anywhere through the library portal, using the university login.

6. Rules inside the library
Silence must be kept in the reading hall. Food is not allowed. Mobile phones must be on silent.

7. Contact
Librarian: Dr. Neha Bansal (fictional), Central Library, ground floor. Email: library@example.edu
