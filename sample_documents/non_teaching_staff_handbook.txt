NON-TEACHING STAFF HANDBOOK - 2026-27

1. Working hours and shifts
General shift: 9:00 am to 5:30 pm, Monday to Friday, with a lunch break from 1:00 pm to 1:30 pm.
Morning shift (security and transport): 6:00 am to 2:00 pm.
Evening shift (security and transport): 2:00 pm to 10:00 pm.
Saturday duty is by roster, once a month, and gives one compensatory day off.

2. Overtime
Overtime is paid at Rs 250 per hour, for at most 30 hours a month, and must be approved in advance by the section supervisor.

3. Leave
Casual leave: 12 days per year.
Earned leave: 18 days per year.
Medical leave: 10 days per year with a medical certificate.

4. Uniform allowance
Staff in security, transport and housekeeping receive a uniform allowance of Rs 6,000 per year, paid in April.

5. Staff ID card
The staff ID card must be renewed every year before 30 June at the Administration Office, Room 8. A lost card is replaced for Rs 300.

6. Grievances
Grievances can be raised with the Staff Welfare Officer, Administrative Block, Room 15, or by email: staff.welfare@example.edu
